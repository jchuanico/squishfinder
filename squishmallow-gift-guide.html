Squishmallow gift FAQ

Which Squishmallow should I buy as a gift?
Match it to the person: shy and sweet friends tend to love cozy squishes like Hans the Hedgehog or Cam the Cat, while bold personalities suit Gordon the Shark or Leonard the Lion. If you're unsure, take the free 6-question gift quiz and answer for them.
What size Squishmallow makes the best gift?
Squishmallows commonly come in 5", 8", 12", 16" and 24" sizes. Small ones (5" to 8") fit in a stocking or gift bag, 12" is a classic main gift, and 16" to 24" makes a big huggable pillow. Check the size in the listing before you buy.
What if they already have a big collection?
Pick a squish that matches their personality or favorite color, or look for limited editions. Collectors look for silver (Rare), gold (Ultra Rare) and black (Select Series) tags, so check the tag photo in the listing.
