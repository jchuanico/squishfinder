Meet the dumplings

Mystery squishy dumplings come hidden in tiny steamer baskets, so every one is a surprise. They're a fun stocking stuffer, and there are advent calendars with 24 of them too.

⚠️ Squishy safety: never heat, microwave or freeze a squishy, even if you've seen it on TikTok. Don't cut or poke them, throw away any that leak, and keep small ones away from kids under 3.
