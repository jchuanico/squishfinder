What sizes do Squishmallows come in?

Common sizes are 5", 8", 12", 16" and 24". Small ones (5" to 8") fit in a stocking or backpack, 12" is the classic gift size, and 16" to 24" makes a big huggable pillow.

How can you tell if a Squishmallow is rare?
