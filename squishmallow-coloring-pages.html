Colouring ideas

  • Copy the real colours from each squish's profile (tap See colours), or invent your own rainbow version.
  • Add patterns like stripes, spots, hearts or stars, and draw a background: a bedroom, a beach, a snowy day.
  • Party idea: print a stack, let everyone colour their quiz match, then vote for the cutest one.

Squishmallow coloring page FAQ

Are these Squishmallow coloring pages free?
Yes. All 24 colouring sheets are free to print at home or at school, with no sign-up. They're for personal and classroom use.
Can I colour a Squishmallow online?
Yes. Tap "Colour it" under any squish to paint it in your browser: tap an area with the fill bucket, or use the brush and eraser. You can use the squish's real plush colours or any colour you like, then save your picture or print it. Nothing is uploaded.
How do I print a Squishmallow coloring page?
Tap Print under the squish you want and choose your printer, or "Save as PDF" to print later. Tap "Print all 24" to get every sheet, one squish per page.
What age are these colouring pages for?
The big, simple shapes suit kids from about 3 to 10, and older fans like them too. Little ones can use crayons and older kids can add patterns with markers or pencil crayons.
Are these official Squishmallows pictures?
No. They're original fan drawings by Squish Finder, a fan-made site that isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Jazwares or Squishmallows.
