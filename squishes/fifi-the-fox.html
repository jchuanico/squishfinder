Collector’s corner: rarity, editions and trading cards

Fifi’s standard plush is a regular release, so it's usually easy to find in stores and online.

How Squishmallows rarity works

Most Squishmallows are standard releases. Limited runs are marked on the tag: collectors look for silver (Rare), gold (Ultra Rare) and black (Select Series) tags, plus event exclusives (like Comic-Con editions) and limited colors. Check the tag photo in a listing before paying a collector price.

Trading cards

Collect cards too? Panini makes official Squishmallows trading cards (Series 1 in 2024 and Series 2 in 2025). Look for Fifi on the set checklists and keep them next to your plush collection.
