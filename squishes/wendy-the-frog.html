Collector’s corner: rarity, editions and trading cards

  • Wendy is one of the original Squishmallows (collector number 6).
  • A gray 8-inch Wendy from San Diego Comic-Con 2022 is a well-known rare edition.
  • Wendy also comes as a tiny 2.5-inch 'MallowMees figure.

How Squishmallows rarity works

Most Squishmallows are standard releases. Limited runs are marked on the tag: collectors look for silver (Rare), gold (Ultra Rare) and black (Select Series) tags, plus event exclusives (like Comic-Con editions) and limited colors. Check the tag photo in a listing before paying a collector price.

Trading cards

Collect cards too? Panini makes official Squishmallows trading cards (Series 1 in 2024 and Series 2 in 2025). Look for Wendy on the set checklists and keep them next to your plush collection.
